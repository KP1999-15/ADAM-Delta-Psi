ADAM

The First of Generation 7
StatusActive
ClassificationSynthetic Enhanced
OriginGeneration 7
Observer DensityHighest Recorded

Adam is the first fully synthetic Enhanced human being, created as proof that extraordinary power can be engineered.

He possesses the highest observer density ever recorded. But the real question surrounding Adam is not how powerful he can become. It is whether someone created rather than born can learn how to be human.
